About

I am a scientist working at the intersection of bioelectrochemistry, analytical instrumentation, and pharmaceutical quality systems. My work focuses on measurement science within GMP environments, including HPLC and GC calibration, method validation, and system qualification.

I am particularly interested in how rigorous analytical frameworks translate into real-world pharmaceutical development and regulatory decision-making.
